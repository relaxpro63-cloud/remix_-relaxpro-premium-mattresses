## 1. Introduction

### What is Sanity CMS?

Sanity CMS is a **Content Management System** — a tool that lets you edit your website's content without writing any code. Think of it as a control panel for your website where you can change text, swap images, add new products, and update offers just by filling in forms.

### How to Log In

1. Open your web browser and go to: **https://relaxpro.sanity.studio**
2. Click **"Continue with Google"**
3. Sign in with the Google account that was given access to the studio
4. You will see the main dashboard with a sidebar on the left

### Basic Navigation

- **Left sidebar**: Lists all types of content you can edit (Pages, Products, Offers, etc.)
- **Main area**: Shows the content you selected from the sidebar
- **Top bar**: Shows your user name and a "Publish" button

### Draft vs Published

- **Draft**: Changes you've made but haven't made public yet (saved automatically as you type)
- **Published**: Content that is currently visible on your live website
- You can edit a published document — the website still shows the old version until you click **Publish**

### How to Undo Mistakes

- If you made a change but haven't published yet, just close the tab without saving
- If you published a mistake, you can restore a previous version:
  1. Open the document
  2. Click the clock icon (⏱) in the top bar
  3. Select a previous version to restore
  4. Click "Publish" to make it live

> ⚠️ **Always publish your changes for them to appear on the website!** Saving alone does not make content visible.

## 5. Managing Products

**Where:** Left sidebar → **🛏️ Mattresses**

### 5.1 Creating a New Product

1. Click **"Mattresses"** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"Create New"** button (top right, ➕ icon)
3. Fill in the fields (see below for each field explanation)
4. Upload images
5. Set SEO (optional)
6. Click **"Publish"** to make it visible on the website

### Product Fields Explained

#### Basic Info

| Field | Required? | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Product Name** | ✅ Yes | Full product name | "Amrita Pure Latex Mattress" |
| **Slug** | ✅ Yes | URL address (auto-generated from name) | `amrita-pure-latex-mattress` |
| **Tagline** | No | Short catchy phrase | "Nature's Finest Sleep" |
| **Subtitle** | No | Second line below name | "100% Natural Dunlop Latex" |
| **Key Benefit** | No | Main selling point (1-2 sentences) | "Orthopedic support with natural cooling properties" |
| **Description** | No | Full product description | ... |
| **Badge** | No | Small label shown on product card | "Bestseller" or "New" |

#### Comfort & Dimensions

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| **Comfort Level** | Choose from: Firm, Medium-Firm, Medium, Medium-Soft, Soft-Medium, Plush |
| **Comfort Rating (1-5)** | Number from 1 (firmest) to 5 (softest) |
| **Total Thickness (inches)** | Overall mattress height |

#### Internal Architecture (Layers)

This is where you describe what's inside the mattress. Click "Add item" to add each layer.

Each layer has:
- **Thickness (inches)** — How thick this layer is (e.g., 2, 4, 6)
- **Material** — Type (Rebonded Foam, Latex, HR Foam, etc.)
- **Brand** — Brand name (e.g., "Dunlop")
- **Certifications** — Any certifications (e.g., "GOLS", "OEKO-TEX")
- **Description** — What this layer does

> 💡 **Layer order matters!** The top layer in the list = the top layer inside the mattress.

#### Pricing

Choose the pricing model first:

- **With / Without Accessories** — Shows two prices: with pillows+protector, or mattress only
- **Fabric Options** — Shows prices for 300GSM vs 450GSM fabric

Then fill in prices for each size (King, Queen, Double, Single, Diwan, Custom).

> ⚠️ **Prices are in INR (₹).** Do not include commas — just numbers.

#### Features & Benefits

- **Features** — Bullet points shown on product cards (e.g., "GOLS Certified", "10-Year Warranty")
- **Key Benefits** — List of benefits shown on the product page

#### Images

- **Product Images** — Main gallery (first image is the primary one). Upload multiple images.
- **Thumbnail** — Small card preview image (if empty, uses the first gallery image)

#### Categories & Tags

- **Category** — Which product category this belongs to (must be created first under **Categories**)
- **Tier** — Comfort, Premium, or Luxury
- **Tags** — Optional keywords for filtering

#### Flags

Toggle these on/off:
- **Bestseller** — Shows a "Bestseller" badge
- **New Arrival** — Shows a "New" badge
- **Featured on Homepage** — Makes it available for homepage selection
- **In Stock** — Uncheck to hide from the website

#### SEO

See the [SEO Settings](#13-seo-settings) section for details.

### 5.2 Editing an Existing Product

1. Click **"Mattresses"** in the sidebar
2. Find the product in the list (use search if needed)
3. Click on the product name to open it
4. Make your changes
5. Click **"Publish"**

### 5.3 Hiding a Product (Without Deleting)

1. Open the product
2. Scroll down to **"In Stock"**
3. **Uncheck** the box
4. Click **"Publish"**

The product will no longer appear on the website, but all its data is preserved.

### 5.4 Deleting a Product

1. Open the product
2. Click the **three dots (⋯)** in the top-right
3. Select **"Delete"**
4. Confirm

> ⚠️ **Be careful!** Deleting a product is permanent. If it's linked to other content (testimonials, homepage), those links will break.

## 6. Creating & Managing Offers

**Where:** Left sidebar → **🎉 Offers & Campaigns**

Offers appear as an announcement banner at the top of every page on your website. They're great for seasonal discounts, festive sales, and promotional campaigns.

### Step-by-Step: Creating a New Offer

1. Click **"Offers & Campaigns"** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **"Create New"** button (➕ icon, top right)
3. Fill in the following fields:

#### Step 1: Basic Information

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer Title** | Name of your offer | "Independence Day Sale" |
| **Subtitle** | Short description shown on the banner | "Celebrate freedom with better sleep" |
| **Description** | Full details (shown when expanded) | "Get 15% off on all Pure Latex mattresses..." |
| **Badge Text** | Small label on the banner | "Limited Time" or "Festive Offer" |

#### Step 2: Offer Details

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer Type** | Type of promotion | "Discount Offer" |
| **Discount Text** | Bold text shown on banner | "15% OFF" or "FLAT ₹5000 OFF" |
| **Coupon Code** | Code customers can enter at checkout | "FREEDOM15" |
| **Banner Image** | Optional visual banner image | ... |

#### Step 3: Call to Action (CTA)

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **CTA Button → Button Text** | What the button says | "Shop Now" |
| **CTA Button → Link URL** | Where it goes | `/catalog` |
| **CTA Button → Style Variant** | Button style | Primary (green) |
| **CTA Button → Open in New Tab** | Open link in new tab | Usually unchecked |

#### Step 4: Timing & Visibility

| Field | What to enter | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Start Date** | When the offer begins | 2026-07-28 00:00 |
| **End Date** | When the offer ends | 2026-08-20 23:59 |
| **Active** | Must be checked to show on website | ✅ Checked |
| **Show as Announcement Banner** | Shows at the top of every page | ✅ Checked |
| **Banner Color** | Color scheme | "Brand" (blue) |
| **Display Priority** | Higher numbers show first | 100 |
| **Target Products** | Optional: link to specific products | Leave empty or select products |

#### Step 5: Publish

4. Click **"Publish"** to make the offer live

### How to Tell If an Offer Is Showing

An active offer with:
- ✅ **Active** = checked
- ✅ **Show as Announcement Banner** = checked
- ✅ **End Date** is in the future (or empty)
- ✅ **Priority** set (higher = shown first)

...will appear as a colored banner at the top of your website.

### Editing an Existing Offer

1. Click **"Offers & Campaigns"** in the sidebar
2. Find and click the offer you want to edit
3. Make your changes (e.g., extend the end date, change the discount)
4. Click **"Publish"**

### Ending / Disabling an Offer

To remove an offer from the website without deleting it:

1. Open the offer
2. Uncheck **"Active"**
3. Click **"Publish"**

The offer banner will disappear. You can re-enable it later by checking "Active" again.

### Deleting an Offer

1. Open the offer
2. Click the three dots (⋯) → **"Delete"**
3. Confirm

## 12. Uploading Images

### Supported Formats

| Format | Best For |
|---|---|
| **PNG** | Logos, graphics with transparent backgrounds |
| **JPEG / JPG** | Photos, product images |
| **WebP** | Best performance (converted automatically) |

### Recommended Image Sizes

| Where | Recommended Size |
|---|---|
| **Product gallery images** | 1200×800px |
| **Product thumbnail** | 400×400px |
| **Hero / Banner images** | 1920×800px |
| **Logo** | 500×500px or larger (PNG with transparency) |
| **Showroom photos** | 1200×800px |
| **Customer photos (testimonials)** | 200×200px square |

### How to Upload

1. Click the image field area (usually a dashed box or placeholder)
2. You can:
   - **Drag & drop** an image file from your computer
   - **Click** to browse and select a file
   - **Paste** an image from clipboard
3. After upload, you can:
   - **Crop** — Drag the circle/square to set the focal point (hotspot)
   - **Add Alt Text** — Describe what's in the image (important for accessibility)
   - **Add Caption** — Optional text shown below the image

### Replacing an Image

1. Click on the existing image
2. Click **"Replace"** or drag a new file
3. The old image will be replaced

### Best Practices for Image Alt Text

Good alt text examples:
- ✅ "100% Natural Dunlop Latex mattress with organic cotton cover"
- ✅ "RelaxPro factory showroom in Hyderabad, Jeedimetla"
- ✅ "GOLS Certified Organic Latex certification badge"

Bad alt text:
- ❌ "Image" (too vague)
- ❌ "img_2024.jpg" (auto filename)

2. Click it

4. It may take 1-5 minutes for changes to appear due to caching

- After clicking "Publish," look for the clock icon (⏱)

- The content will automatically publish at that time

## 17. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why can't I publish?

A: A required field is likely empty. Look for red borders or asterisks (*). Common missing fields are: Product Name, Slug, Offer Title, or Customer Name.

### Q: Can I undo changes after publishing?

A: Yes! Open the document, click the clock icon (⏱) in the top bar, select a previous version, and publish it.

### Q: How do I change an image on the website?

A: Go to the section where the image appears (e.g., Product, Site Settings → Static Images, Hero Section), click on the image, upload a new one, and click Publish.

### Q: How do I create a new product?

A: Go to Mattresses in the sidebar → Create New → fill in all fields → upload images → set pricing → Publish. See [Creating a New Product](#51-creating-a-new-product) for full details.

### Q: How do I add an offer / discount banner?

A: Go to Offers & Campaigns → Create New → fill in title, discount, dates → set "Show as Announcement Banner" = Yes → Publish. See [Creating & Managing Offers](#6-creating--managing-offers).

### Q: How do I remove an old offer?

A: Open the offer → uncheck "Active" → Publish. The banner will disappear. You can also set the end date to yesterday and publish.

### Q: How do I hide a product without deleting it?

A: Open the product → uncheck "In Stock" → Publish. The product won't appear on the website but all data is preserved.

### Q: How do I reorder items on a page?

A: Use the "Display Order" or "Sort Order" field — lower numbers appear first. Products also have a sort order in their individual settings. Many sections also allow drag-and-drop reordering.

### Q: Why is my WhatsApp number not showing?

A: Check Site Settings → Contact Info → WhatsApp Number. Make sure it's filled in. Also check if the format is correct (e.g., +918686624494).

### Q: Why aren't my social media links working?

A: Go to Site Settings → Footer → Social Links. Check that each link has the correct full URL (starting with https://).

### Q: How do I add a new showroom location?

A: Go to Showrooms → Create New → fill in name, address, hours, contact info → Publish. Then add it to the Contact Page showroom selection.

### Q: Can I schedule an offer to start automatically?

A: Yes! Use the Start Date and End Date fields. The offer will automatically appear during that period (as long as "Active" is checked).

### Q: How do I change the homepage hero image?

A: Go to Pages → Hero Section → click on the slide → click the image → upload a new one → Publish.

### Q: What's the difference between Draft and Published?

A: Draft = work in progress (only you can see it). Published = visible to everyone on the website. Changes only appear after clicking Publish.

### Q: The website looks different from what I published. Why?

A: The website uses caching to load faster. Changes may take 1-5 minutes to appear. Try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R).
